About Me

Gion Terrence Pozon is a third-year Computer Engineering student at Jose Rizal University. His academic focus spans artificial intelligence, embedded systems, and software development, with hands-on experience building intelligent systems for real-world applications.

He has worked on projects ranging from image classification using convolutional neural networks to IoT-based physical systems, and he is experienced in deploying machine learning models to mobile platforms. He approaches engineering problems with a practical mindset, prioritizing solutions that are functional, efficient, and impactful.

Outside the classroom, he participates in programming competitions and continues to develop his technical skills independently. He is driven by a commitment to grow as an engineer and to contribute meaningfully to the field of computing.
